The Hanover Doctoral Research

Dr. Sokar’s doctoral thesis at the Hochschule für Musik, Theater und Medien Hannover, "The Modern German School of Flute Pedagogy: A Cross-Cultural Investigation", investigated historical and acoustic foundations of tone production across international schools of flute playing.

Embouchure & Overtones

By managing air-stream velocity, splitting angle across the embouchure hole, and oral cavity resonance, the flutist can selectively amplify specific partials without forcing the fundamental pitch.

DAW & Audio Applications

Combining classical woodwind knowledge with modern studio engineering allows Dr. Sokar to guide students in acoustic recording, microphone placement, equalization, and multi-track mixing.
